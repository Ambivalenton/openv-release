## Independent project

Öpen-V is not an official app of a transport operator or data provider. Öpen-V uses VRR OpenService, with a focus on Rhine-Ruhr and services returned by that system. Nationwide local stop search does not establish nationwide transit coverage. There is no account, ticket sale or booking.

Transit information is supplied by Verkehrsverbund Rhein-Ruhr (VRR), without warranty. [Source and terms](https://www.opendata-oepnv.de/ht/de/api).

## Guidance and limits

Timetables, forecasts, maps and weather may be incomplete or inaccurate. Missing realtime information does not confirm punctuality. Location and movement do not prove boarding or the correct platform. Check station displays, announcements and operator instructions. Walking routes are not certified accessible routes. These notices do not restrict mandatory statutory rights or liability.
