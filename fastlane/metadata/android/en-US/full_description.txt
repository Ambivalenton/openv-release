Your journey does not have to look like a spreadsheet.

Öpen-V turns your next connections into readable sentences. Choose the building blocks: departure, arrival, stops, duration, changes, walking sections or an optional weather hint for your destination. Spend less time searching and get a clearer picture of the journeys you actually take.

• Your places: save destinations with your own names, icons and colours. Time rules can highlight or temporarily hide them.
• Your connections: search from your location or a manual origin. A timeline, list, intermediate stops and realtime predictions help you plan where provider data is available.
• Your departure board: choose saved stops and personalise 16 designs, from Material to flip dots, split flaps and e-paper.
• Your widgets: quick destinations and a departure board on your Android home screen.
• Your style: 11 app themes, custom colours, light and dark appearances, and AMOLED.
• On the go: pin journeys, explore maps and open walking directions externally with OpenStreetMap.
• A little company: 989 offline facts, thoughts, free-form travel haiku and satire entries and optional Open-Meteo weather hints.
• German and English follow your Android language; other languages fall back to English.

Öpen-V is 100 % vibecoded: its own app code was created with AI assistance. Ambivalenton originally wanted a tool for personal everyday journeys. Over time, other people asked to use it too. That interest became this free software release. There is no AI service running in the app.

No account, ads, tracking SDKs or Google Play Services. Saved places and settings stay on your device. Transit, place-search, map and weather requests send the necessary query information and your IP address to the selected providers. Weather starts switched off. Widgets request no additional background-location permission. A manual origin works without location permission.

Where does it work? Öpen-V uses VRR OpenService, focused on Rhine-Ruhr. Complete German or cross-border coverage is not guaranteed. Worldwide place search does not imply worldwide transit routing.

New journeys need internet. Realtime data may be missing; old board departures are not an offline timetable. Android may delay widget updates. No ticket sales or verified accessible-route planning. Open-Meteo’s free hosted API is limited to non-commercial use.

Free software under GPL-3.0-or-later. An independent project, not an official transport operator app. Transit results rely on external, partly proprietary upstream systems.

Live journey guidance: pin a connection paragraph to see your next step, disruptions and reachable alternatives in a focus dialog and local notification. The app attempts an update approximately every minute; Android and the provider may delay updates.


Transit data: VRR. Information is supplied without guarantee.
